Camel Pay

A simple, secure way to move money between Camel Wallet accounts.

How it works

  1. Enter the account number you want to pay.
  2. Search the name to confirm it is the right person.
  3. Enter the amount and your PIN, then send.

Why Camel Pay

  • Instant transfers between Camel Wallet accounts
  • The account name is checked before any money moves
  • Every payment is protected by your 4-digit PIN
  • A full history of everything you send and receive
